03 — Product

One engine. Four kinds of engineering work.

Each module reads a different part of the record, and every result points back to the file, the view or the feature it came from.

01 / CAD

CAD Intelligence

Understand STEP geometry and assemblies as features, not triangles — holes, bores, faces and the intent behind them.

  • Feature recognition on B-rep
  • Mass, volume, envelope
  • Parametric, editable output
02 / Drawings

Drawing Intelligence

Extract dimensions, tolerances, GD&T and notes from the drawing — including scanned sheets with no selectable text.

  • Ballooned characteristics
  • Tolerance & GD&T parsing
  • Drawing ↔ model cross-check
03 / Assemblies

Assembly Intelligence

Cross-check interfaces, BOMs and mating relationships before parts are cut — clearance, fasteners, hole alignment.

  • Interface & mate checks
  • BOM ↔ CAD reconciliation
  • Fastener & clearance rules
04 / Documents

Engineering Documentation

Generate inspection reports, FAI drafts, revision analysis and traceability — ready for an engineer to review and sign.

  • AS9102 Forms 1, 2 & 3
  • Revision-to-revision delta
  • Source on every value
